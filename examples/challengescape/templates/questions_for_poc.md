# Questions for the challenge point of contact — Lab NN

Asked from a position of "we built the simplest credible loop; tell us where
it's wrong," not "we solved your problem." 5 questions, each one of:

1. <Which real dataset/series should replace the offline stand-in first?>
2. <What is the real operating constraint (false-alarm rate, cost ratio,
   granularity) that the synthetic proxy pinned arbitrarily?>
3. <What threshold makes the result actionable in your setting?>
4. <Which axis of the design is the bigger open question?>
5. Would a standing, provenance-tracked lab like this be useful to your
   group — one that reruns automatically as new data arrives and writes a
   reviewed memo when the answer changes?

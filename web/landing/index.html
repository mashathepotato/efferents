The job split
YOU / THESIS / STEERING

You choose where to look. Efferents keeps looking.

Set the thesis, falsifier, constraints, and budget. The lab reads, proposes, runs, compares, and preserves the evidence for your review.

You keep the judgment. The lab takes the repetition.

Why now

Research throughput now compounds.

The advantage is not replacing researchers. It is giving good researchers more rigorous iterations.

Manual only

Every sweep, evaluation, and review waits for your attention.

One loop

Routine work keeps moving while you write, teach, meet, or sleep.

Lab network

Independent loops test more directions and exchange evidence across questions.

Automate one part to keep up. Connect auditable loops to get ahead.

How it works
  1. Frame a falsifiable question around your repository and metrics.
  2. The lab reads, plans, runs, evaluates, and records provenance.
  3. Inspect the evidence. Steer, redirect, pause, or stop.
